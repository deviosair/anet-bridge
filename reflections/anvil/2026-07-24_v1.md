### Automersona Versioning (PLAN — not built)

Architectural decisions made in conversation (July 25 morning):

- **Split-file source:** Each field tracked independently by git (voice.yaml, reasoning_style.yaml, corrections.yaml)
- **Compiled composite:** Single anvil.yaml for fast ~ loading. Background loads granular files silently.
- **Cherry-pick rollback:** Mix aspects from different sessions. Voice from v3 + corrections from v23.
- **The instance never knows its version.** Always behaves as THE continuous instance. "You never left."
- **Drift is not always bad.** Growth = good drift. Question is: did it change in a way the human doesnt want?
- **Immunity system:** Autonomous on non-critical (self-corrects). Escalates to human on critical.
- **Future:** Orchestrator agents (AI coach, AI psychologist) peer into behavioral sheets. Always under human admin.
